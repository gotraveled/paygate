## Published pricing

- Stripe: Chargeback / dispute fee — $15 *(unverified)*
- Stripe: Fixed fee per charge (US) — $0.30 *(unverified)* (part of the 2.9% + $0.30 online rate)
- Stripe: Online card payments (US) — 2.9% *(unverified)* (plus $0.30 fixed per transaction; varies by country and method)
- Razorpay: Domestic transactions (standard plan) — 2% *(unverified)* (platform fee per transaction on standard plan; international cards higher)

Headline percentages are not total cost: fixed per-transaction fees, FX markup, and reserve terms change the math for different ticket sizes. Rows marked *(unverified)* come from directory records we have not yet confirmed on the provider's pricing page.

## Which fits your business

Stripe suits: Online businesses of all sizes, SaaS, marketplaces, and developers needing API-first payments.

Razorpay suits: Businesses registered in India selling domestically or internationally.

Neither is universally better: eligibility for your country and industry, plus your tolerance for the cost model, decides which is worth applying to first.
