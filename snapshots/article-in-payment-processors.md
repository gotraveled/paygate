## Business formation services

These are not payment processors — they form and maintain business entities (India- or foreign-owned). If a provider above requires an entity type you do not have, a formation service may be the prerequisite step.

- **[Stripe Atlas](/providers/stripe-atlas)** — Delaware company formation (C-corp or LLC) for global founders, from Stripe.
- **[Firstbase](/providers/firstbase)** — Company formation and compliance platform for global founders.
- **[doola](/providers/doola)** — US LLC/C-corp formation with bookkeeping and compliance add-ons for global founders.
- **[Clerky](/providers/clerky)** — Legal formation service used heavily by startups for Delaware incorporations.
- **[Northwest Registered Agent](/providers/northwest-registered-agent)** — Registered agent and LLC/corporation formation in every US state.
- **[Bizee](/providers/bizee)** — Low-cost US LLC formation (formerly Incfile) with a free basic tier.

## How to choose

- **If a direct fit exists** (7 providers), start there — apply to the one whose industry list and fee model match your business.
- **If everything is conditional**, the deciding factor is usually entity type and underwriting tolerance, not price.
- **Verify before signing**: a provider's presence on a supported-country page does not guarantee approval; underwriting still applies.

Check each provider's profile for the evidence-linked eligibility row before applying.
